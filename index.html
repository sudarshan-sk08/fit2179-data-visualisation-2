A World on Fire

Bushfires, or Wildfires as they are more commonly known globally are events that affect almost every country. Australia is not alone in facing increasing fire risk, but it does see itself consistently ranking among the world's most fire-affected nations. This map shows the average area burned per wildfire event by country.
